Questions, answered

Everything about ordering, fit, delivery and returns. Can’t find it? Ask us directly.

Shopping

How do I place an order?

Add pieces to your bag, then send your bag to us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and payment details with you directly.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Mobile money, bank transfer and cash on delivery within the city. We confirm the options when we reply to your order.

Sizing

How do your sizes run?

Most pieces fit true to size. Tailored items such as blazers are cut close, so choose the larger size if you are between two.

Can you help me choose a size?

Yes. Message us your height and usual size and we will recommend the best fit.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Within the city, one to two working days. To other regions, three to five working days.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is free on orders over 50,000 XAF. Below that, the fee depends on your location and is confirmed before you pay.

Returns and exchanges

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unworn, with its tags attached.

Can I exchange for another size or colour?

Yes, subject to stock. Contact us within 7 days and we will arrange the swap.

Customer support

How do I reach you?

By WhatsApp, phone or email during opening hours. We reply to every message within one working day.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will replace the item or refund you.

Our story

We started with a rail of eight dresses and a shared mirror. Friends came for the fit and stayed for the advice, and the advice became the business.

Today we choose every piece ourselves, from tailored blazers to everyday sneakers. If we would not wear it to a wedding, a workday and a Sunday lunch, it does not make the rail.

Our philosophy is quiet confidence: strong shapes, honest fabrics and a palette you can mix without thinking.

To help every customer dress as the person they are becoming.

That is our mission. Every fitting, every message and every parcel is judged against it.

What we stand for

Craft

We pick pieces with clean seams, good lining and fabric that holds its shape after many washes.

Confidence

Fit comes first. We help you find the size and cut that make you stand taller.

Honesty

Fair prices, clear sale tags and plain answers about fabric, fit and delivery.

Community

We style local events, work with local tailors and answer every message ourselves.
